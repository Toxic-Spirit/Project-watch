Frequently Asked Questions

How do I track my order?

Log in to your account and click "View My Orders & Track" from your profile page. You will see a real-time status update for every order you have placed.

What is the return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for unworn timepieces in original packaging. Contact our support team to initiate a return.

Are all watches genuine and authenticated?

Yes. Every timepiece listed on DeaLon is verified for authenticity by our in-house horological experts before being listed for sale.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept Credit Cards, Debit Cards, UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM), and Cash on Delivery for eligible orders.

How long does delivery take?

Standard delivery takes 5–7 business days. Express delivery (2–3 business days) is available at checkout for an additional charge.

Warranty & Service

All timepieces sold through DeaLon come with the manufacturer's international warranty. Additionally, DeaLon provides a 1-year authenticity guarantee on all purchases. For servicing, polishing, or strap replacements, contact our service centre at service@dealon.com.
