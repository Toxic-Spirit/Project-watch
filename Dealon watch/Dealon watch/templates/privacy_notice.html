Privacy Notice

How we handle your data

Last updated: January 2025

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, including your name, email address, phone number, shipping address, and payment information. We also collect information about your browsing activity, search queries, and purchases on our platform.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process orders, send order confirmations and tracking updates, provide customer support, personalise your shopping experience, and communicate important account and service information. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information. Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt hashing. Payment details are processed securely and are never stored on our servers in plain text.

4. Cookies

We use cookies to maintain your session, remember your login preference, and improve site performance. You can disable cookies through your browser settings, however this may affect certain features of the platform.

5. Third-Party Services

We may share necessary data with trusted third-party service providers such as payment processors and delivery partners solely for the purpose of fulfilling your order. These partners are bound by their own privacy policies and are prohibited from using your data for any other purpose.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data held by DeaLon. To exercise these rights, contact us at privacy@dealon.com. We will respond to your request within 30 days.

7. Contact

For any privacy-related queries, contact our Data Protection Officer at privacy@dealon.com or write to us at DeaLon Watches Pvt. Ltd., 12th Floor, Prestige Tower, MG Road, Bengaluru — 560001.
