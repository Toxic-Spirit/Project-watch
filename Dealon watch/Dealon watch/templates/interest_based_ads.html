Interest-Based Ads

Our Advertising Policy

Last updated: January 2025

What Are Interest-Based Ads?

Interest-based advertising (also known as personalised advertising) allows us to show you advertisements that are more relevant to your interests. These ads are based on your browsing behaviour, search history, and purchase activity on the DeaLon platform.

How We Use Your Data for Ads

We may use information such as the watches you viewed, brands you searched for, and purchases you made to personalise the recommendations and promotions shown to you on DeaLon. This data is used solely within our platform and is not shared with external advertisers.

No Third-Party Ad Networks

DeaLon does not participate in third-party advertising networks. We do not allow external advertisers to place ads on our platform or access your personal data for advertising purposes. All promotions you see on DeaLon are from verified sellers on our platform only.

Your Choices

You can opt out of interest-based recommendations by adjusting your account preferences. Note that opting out does not mean you will see no promotions — it means the promotions shown will not be tailored to your individual behaviour.

Contact Us

For questions about our advertising policy, contact us at support@dealon.com.
