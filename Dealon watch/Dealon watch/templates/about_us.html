Our Story

DeaLon was founded in 2025 with a single vision — to create India's most trusted destination for luxury timepieces. We believe that a great watch is more than an accessory; it is a statement of character, craftsmanship, and legacy.

From the iconic Submariner of Rolex to the gravity-defying complications of Richard Mille, every piece in our collection is hand-selected by our team of horological experts. We partner directly with authorised dealers and certified sellers to guarantee authenticity on every single purchase.
